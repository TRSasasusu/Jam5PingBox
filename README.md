# Diorama

Lore of a Nomai named Ping. Explore why there are modding jams in the Outer Wilds universe.

An entry for Outer Wilds modding Jam 5.

## Notes for judges

**The jam submission version is Diorama v0.4.1 and Mod Jam 5 v1.0.3.**

There seem to be two issues, but I have not encountered them (is Wyrm cursed?):

* Broken tractor beams: I haven't seen this. But Ixrec and Wyrm have seen it. It seems not to occur when you play Diorama v0.4.1 and Mod Jam 5 v1.0.3, but I don't know. I haven't seen it.
* The broken size of the hatchling: I haven't seen this, either. But Wyrm has seen it when they used the first warp pad from the central station to the diorama interface. I suspect the small warp pad might cause such a phenomenon, but I don't know because only Wyrm has encountered this. It could be fixed by the meditation.
